CedarFlow Studio is a fictional workflow automation business based in Beirut, Lebanon, serving independent service businesses and small teams such as tutoring centers, design studios, and home-service providers. Its mission is to give small businesses more time for their customers by simplifying repetitive administrative work with practical automation. Its tagline is "Less busywork. More business."

CedarFlow offers four services: workflow discovery and mapping; customer inquiry and lead tracking; appointment reminders and follow-up workflows; and simple business dashboards. It helps clients organize incoming inquiries, prepare follow-up messages for staff approval, send reminders for appointments already confirmed by staff, and summarize information such as open inquiries and upcoming appointments. Each project begins with a review of the client's process and existing tools, followed by an agreed scope, a pilot, and a handover with documentation and staff training.

The fictional founding team consists of Maya Haddad, Co-founder and Business Operations Lead, who maps processes and coordinates client onboarding; Karim Nasser, Co-founder and Automation Engineer, who builds integrations and tests workflows; and Leila Mansour, Customer Success and Training Lead, who prepares guides, trains staff, and gathers feedback. The business serves clients remotely across Lebanon, with in-person meetings in Beirut by arrangement. Client communication and training are available in English and Arabic.

CedarFlow's unique value proposition is a small, clearly scoped automation project built around tools the client already uses, paired with a documented handover so staff can operate the workflow confidently. It prioritizes useful improvements, clear ownership, and human approval for customer-facing decisions. It does not promise revenue gains or offer legal, medical, or financial advice. Pricing is provided through a custom quote after discovery; there is no published price list or guaranteed delivery timeline. Ongoing support is available only when included in the agreed project scope.
